EMBER COURT

Duel of the Cindral Throne

Outwit Vex, the Cindral Warden, in a duel of flame, tide, thorn and stone. Spend mana, summon creatures, and bring the Warden's life to zero.

30 life each · mana grows every turn · first to zero loses

Mouse or keyboard — 1–7 pick a card, Enter plays it, E ends your turn

How to play

  • Goal: reduce Vex's life from 30 to 0. If your life reaches 0, you lose.
  • Mana: you gain 1 mana crystal each turn (up to 10) and it refills every turn. Every card costs mana.
  • Creatures: play them onto your board. They cannot attack the turn they are summoned, unless they have Rush.
  • Taunt: a creature with Taunt must be attacked before anything else on its side.
  • Attack: click one of your ready creatures, then click an enemy creature or Vex. Creatures strike back.
  • Spells: Fireball and Ember Surge damage a target; Healing Spring restores your life; Whispering Current draws cards; Thorn Volley and Quake hit every enemy creature.
  • Keyboard: 1–7 select a hand card, Enter play it, E end turn, Esc cancel, R play again after the duel.
